About me

I enjoy figuring out how things work, where they can be improved, and how technology can make them easier to use.

I'm Prinel, a web developer and software engineer with experience across websites, digital systems, CRM, automation and UX.

I tend to be the person who looks at a process and starts asking: why does this take so many steps, where are people getting stuck, and is there a better way to build it?

That has led me to work on everything from website experiences and searchable resource systems to CRM workflows, automation and digital tools designed around real users and real business needs.

I care about the technical side of a project, but I also care about whether the person using it can actually understand it. Accessibility, clarity and thoughtful UX are a big part of how I approach development.

I'm now continuing to grow deeper into software engineering, particularly full-stack development, data and motorsport technology, while building projects that challenge me to learn new tools and solve more complex problems.
