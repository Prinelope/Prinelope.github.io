05 / About

I like understanding how things work, where people get stuck, and how technology can make the process better.

/ approach

I tend to look at a process and start asking questions: why does this take so many steps, what is making it difficult for the user, and is there a clearer way to build it?

I'm Prinel, a web developer and software engineer with professional experience across websites, digital systems, CRM, automation and UX.

My work has included building web experiences, improving resource discovery, designing CRM workflows and connecting systems that would otherwise rely on repetitive manual work.

I care about the technical side of a project, but I also care about whether the person using it can actually understand it. Accessibility, clarity and thoughtful UX are part of how I approach development rather than something I add at the end.

I'm continuing to deepen my software engineering skills through full-stack, data and motorsport technology projects, choosing projects that give me a real problem to investigate rather than building something simply for the sake of adding another project to a portfolio.
